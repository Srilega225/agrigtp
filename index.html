Tomato: Early Blight (Alternaria solani)

Fungal Pathogen • Analyzed in 0.42 seconds
Confidence
Infestation Severity: Moderate Risk (Stage 2)

🔍 Observed Clinical Symptoms

  • Concentric dark brown "target board" rings on older lower leaves.
  • Surrounding yellow chlorotic halos around lesions.
  • Premature defoliation starting from base towards upper foliage.
Zero Chemical Residue Safe for Pollinators
  • Neem Oil Emulsion: Mix 5ml cold-pressed pure neem oil + 2ml liquid soap per liter of water. Spray thoroughly under and over leaves every 5 days.
  • Trichoderma viride Bio-Agent: Apply 5g/liter foliar spray in the evening to outcompete fungal spores naturally.
  • Sanitation: Prune and incinerate lower infected foliage immediately. Never put diseased leaves into compost.
Immediate Intervention Wear Mask & Gloves
  • Fungicide Spray: Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5g per liter OR Chlorothalonil 75% WP @ 2g per liter.
  • Application Timing: Spray early morning when wind speed is under 8 km/h. Do not spray if rain is forecast within 4 hours.
  • Waiting Period (PHI): Maintain a 7-day pre-harvest interval before picking fruits for market.
